WHY NS GLASS

Coastal Durability Meets Architectural Elegance

Founded in Bapatla, NS GLASS has evolved into the region's benchmark architectural glass studio. Coastal Andhra Pradesh poses extreme atmospheric challenges: high humidity, salt spray, and strong wind loads. Standard installations corrode within months; our systems are engineered specifically with SS-316 marine-grade hardware and ISI-certified toughened glass.
