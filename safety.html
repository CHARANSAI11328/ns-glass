Quality & Compliance

Engineered For Safety & Resilience

Every NS GLASS installation adheres strictly to Indian Standards (IS 2553) and coastal engineering tolerances.

IS 2553 Part 1 Certified

Thermal tempering at 680°C produces 5x greater bending strength than annealed glass. In the rare event of breakage, it disintegrates into blunt, harmless granules.

250°C Thermal Shock Resistance

Engineered to handle intense tropical sunlight, sudden coastal rain squalls, and severe temperature variations without micro-fracturing.

SS-316 Coastal Hardware

All hinges, floor springs, spiders, and brackets are forged from molybdenum-enriched SS-316 stainless steel to eliminate coastal saline pit corrosion.

1.5 kN/m Wind Load Rating

Our balcony railings and spider facades are structurally stress-tested to absorb coastal cyclone gusts and intense wind pressure along the Bay of Bengal.

42 dB Sound Isolation

Acoustic PVB laminated interlayers block traffic roar, coastal storms, and office background noise for whisper-quiet interior comfort.

Laser Level Alignment

Our master installation crews calibrate plumb lines using high-precision optical laser levels for smooth door glides and gapless perimeter sealing.
